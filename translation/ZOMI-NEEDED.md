# Zomi: the strings to translate first

Zomi is the one language in the app with no translation at all, so a Zomi
speaker currently reads the whole guide in English.

These fourteen come before the guide content. They are what the app says about
itself: that it is not medical advice, that it is not for emergencies, and what
happens to what you type. A patient who cannot read those is worse off than one
who cannot read a card about heartburn.

The app already bundles the Chin (Hakha) and Burmese screening instruments for
Zomi speakers, on the basis that many read a second language. That does not
help here: a disclaimer only works in a language the reader actually reads.

Fill in the right-hand column and send it back, or paste each one into the
Translation Helper in the app (Tools, last section). Plain everyday Zomi is
what is wanted, not formal or literary register. Keep 911, ACOG and AAP as they
are.
