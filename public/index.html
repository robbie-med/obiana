Your information

What you type stays on this phone. No account, no sign-in, nothing sent. Clearing your browser data erases it, so use Export to keep a copy.

Only two buttons send anything: Help translate and Help Improve This Guide. They send what you wrote, your country, and a scrambled network address to stop spam. Never your name or what is in your trackers.
